The Marking Framework
What is your IA marked against?

Every IB Mathematics Internal Assessment is marked by your teacher against five criteria — A through E — and then externally moderated by the IB. The total mark is out of 20. Criteria A to D are identical for SL and HL students. Criterion E (Use of Mathematics) has separate descriptors for SL and HL, with HL requiring greater sophistication and rigour at the top bands.

A Presentation
B Mathematical Communication
C Personal Engagement
D Reflection
E Use of Mathematics

Marking uses a best-fit model — the descriptor that most accurately reflects the overall level of the work is selected. It is not necessary for every aspect of a descriptor to be met. The documents below are the official and supporting materials that Mathski's marking is based on. Download and review them to understand exactly what examiners are looking for.
