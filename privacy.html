Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information Mathski collects, why, and what we do with it. Questions: mathskiadmin@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your email addressTo send your IA report, to sign you in with a magic link, and to link your purchases to you.
Your account record: role (student or teacher), plan, number of reports used and allowed, plan expiry date, last active date, and the order and customer IDs from our payment providerTo give you the reports you are entitled to.
The IA file you upload, and the course you chooseTo mark it. See "Your IA" below.

We don't collect your name, school or payment card details. Using the Question Bank and Worksheets does not require an account.

Your IA

When you upload an IA, it is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to be marked. Mathski does not save your IA or your report on its own servers. Your report is sent to you by email. Anthropic processes the file under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.

Who processes your information

  • Anthropic: marks your IA.
  • Supabase: stores your account record and handles sign-in.
  • Resend: sends report and sign-in emails.
  • Lemon Squeezy: takes payments as merchant of record. Your card details go to them, not to us.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: serve fonts and code libraries to your browser, which shares your IP address with them.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Cookies and local storage

Mathski does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site stores a few small items in your browser's local storage so it works properly:

  • whether you've seen the welcome message;
  • the email you used for your free report, and whether you've used it.

Signing in stores a session token in your browser. The site loads Lemon Squeezy's checkout script, which may set its own cookies, particularly when you buy something. You can clear all of this in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your account record while you use Mathski. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing mathskiadmin@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Students under 18

Many IB students are under 18. If you are, check with a parent, guardian or teacher before you buy anything or upload work.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
