Refund policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Payments for Mathski are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with.

Student reports (£3.49)

If your report doesn't arrive, or the marking fails for a technical reason, email mathskiadmin@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase. We'll run it again or give you a full refund.

Because a report is delivered as soon as the marking finishes, we can't refund a report that was delivered because you disagree with the marks.

Teacher plan (£49.99 a year)

If you change your mind, email mathskiadmin@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. After 14 days the plan runs until the end of its 12 months and isn't refunded for unused time.

How to ask

Email mathskiadmin@gmail.com from the address you used to buy, with your order number from your Lemon Squeezy receipt. We aim to reply within 3 working days.
