Question 2

\(f''(x)=2x-2\); \(f''(-1)=-4<0\) (max); \(f''(3)=4>0\) (min)

Question 3
Show that \(f(x)=e^x(x-2)\) has a stationary point but no inflection point (where \(f''=0\) is not a sign change).

\(f'(x)=e^x(x-2)+e^x=e^x(x-1)=0\Rightarrow x=1\) (minimum)

\(f''(x)=e^x(x-1)+e^x=e^x\cdot x\). \(f''=0\Rightarrow x=0\). Check sign change: \(f''(-1)=-e^{-1}<0;\;f''(1)=e>0\) — sign changes, so \(x=0\) IS an inflection.
