About Mathski

Last updated 22 October 2026

Mathski is a revision site for IB Mathematics: Analysis & Approaches (AA) and Applications & Interpretation (AI), at Standard and Higher Level.

Who writes it

Everything on Mathski is written by experienced IB teachers and IB curriculum leads. We teach these courses, and we write the questions we want our own students to practise with.

What you'll find here

  • Question Bank. 900 original exam-style questions, sorted by course, level, topic and difficulty, each with a full worked solution. Free.
  • Worksheets. 77 practice worksheets with step-by-step solutions, to print or use on screen. Free.
  • IA Marking. Upload your IA and see a mark for each criterion, A to E, straight away. A full written report costs £3.49.
  • For teachers. IA marking for your classes, with coversheet comments you can edit, for £49.99 a year. Classroom activities are coming soon.

Original, not copied

Our questions are written from scratch. We don't reproduce IB past papers or other publishers' material. You can read how we write and check our questions and how the IA marking works.

Not an IB product

Mathski is independent. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the International Baccalaureate Organization.
