VA \(x=3\), HA \(y=1\), intercepts \((0, frac{1}{3})\) and \((1,0)\)

Question 4
Solve \(\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}>\dfrac{x}{x+1}\).
Rearrange to one side
\(\dfrac{x+1}{x-2}-\dfrac{x}{x+1}>0\Rightarrow\dfrac{(x+1)^2-x(x-2)}{(x-2)(x+1)}>0\Rightarrow\dfrac{3x+1}{(x-2)(x+1)}>0\)
Critical values: \(x=-1,- frac{1}{3},2\)
Sign analysis: positive on \(-12\)
Answer
\(-12\)
Question 5
The graph of \(y=\dfrac{ax+b}{cx+d}\) passes through \((0,2)\) and \((1,3)\), with a vertical asymptote at \(x=-1\). Find \(a,b,c,d\).
VA at \(x=-1\)
\(c(-1)+d=0\Rightarrow d=c\)
Through \((0,2)\)
\( frac{b}{d}=2\Rightarrow b=2d=2c\)
Through \((1,3)\)
\( frac{a+b}{c+d}= frac{a+2c}{2c}=3\Rightarrow a+2c=6c\Rightarrow a=4c\)
Answer
With \(c=1\): \(a=4,b=2,c=1,d=1\), so \(y=\dfrac{4x+2}{x+1}\)
