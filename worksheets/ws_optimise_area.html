Question 1
A rectangle has perimeter 60 cm. Find the dimensions that maximise the area, and state the maximum area.
Let width \(=x\), length \(=30-x\)
\(A=x(30-x)=30x-x^2\)
Maximise
\(A'=30-2x=0\Rightarrow x=15\)
Answer
Square: \(15 imes15\) cm; max area \(=225\) cm²
Question 2
A farmer encloses a rectangular area using a wall on one side and 80 m of fencing for the other three sides. Find the dimensions that maximise the enclosed area.
Let width \(=x\); length \(=80-2x\)
\(A=x(80-2x)\Rightarrow A'=80-4x=0\Rightarrow x=20\)
Answer
\(20 imes40\) m; max area \(=800\) m²
Question 3
A triangle has a base on the \(x\)-axis with vertices at \((-a,0)\), \((a,0)\) and a point \((0,h)\) on the curve \(y=9-x^2\). Find the dimensions of the triangle with maximum area.
Area in terms of \(a\)
\(A= frac{1}{2} imes2a imes(9-a^2)=a(9-a^2)\)
Maximise
\(A'=9-3a^2=0\Rightarrow a=\sqrt{3}\)
Height
\(h=9-3=6\)
Answer
Base \(=2\sqrt{3}\), height \(=6\); max area \(=6\sqrt{3}\) sq units
Question 4
A window consists of a rectangle surmounted by a semicircle. The total perimeter of the window is 10 m. Find the dimensions that maximise the area of the window.
Let rectangle width \(=2r\), height \(=h\); semicircle radius \(=r\)
Perimeter: \(2h+2r+\pi r=10\Rightarrow h= frac{10-r(2+\pi)}{2}\)
Area
\(A=2rh+ frac{\pi r^2}{2}=r(10-r(2+\pi))+ frac{\pi r^2}{2}=10r-r^2(2+\pi)+ frac{\pi r^2}{2}=10r-r^2(2+ frac{\pi}{2})\)
Maximise
\(A'=10-r(4+\pi)=0\Rightarrow r= frac{10}{4+\pi}\)
Answer

Question 5
Find the dimensions of the rectangle of maximum area that can be inscribed in the ellipse \(\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1\).
Parametrise
\(x=4\cos heta,\;y=3\sin heta\); area \(=4xy=48\sin heta\cos heta=24\sin2 heta\)
Maximise
Max when \(\sin2 heta=1\Rightarrow heta= frac{\pi}{4}\Rightarrow x= frac{4}{\sqrt2},\;y= frac{3}{\sqrt2}\)
Answer
Dimensions \(2x imes2y=4\sqrt{2} imes3\sqrt{2}=8 imes6\); max area \(=48\) sq units
