Question 1
A particle moves along a straight line. Its displacement from the origin at time \(t\) seconds is \(s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t+2\), \(t\geq0\).
(a)Find the velocity and acceleration functions.
(b)Find when the particle is stationary.
(c)Find the displacement when \(t=4\).
(d)Find the total distance travelled in the first 4 seconds.
(a)
Answer
\(v(t)=3t^2-12t+9\); \(a(t)=6t-12\)
(b)
\(v=0\)
\(3t^2-12t+9=3(t-1)(t-3)=0\)
Answer
\(t=1\) s and \(t=3\) s
(c)
Answer
\(s(4)=64-96+36+2=6\) m
(d)
Track direction changes
\(s(0)=2;\;s(1)=6;\;s(3)=2;\;s(4)=6\)
Distance
\(|6-2|+|2-6|+|6-2|=4+4+4\)
Answer
12 m
