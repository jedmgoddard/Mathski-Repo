Question 5
When is the median a more appropriate average than the mean? Give an example.
When data is skewed or has outliers
The mean is pulled by extreme values; the median is resistant to outliers.
Example
House prices in a neighbourhood — a few very expensive properties inflate the mean, making the median a better measure of a "typical" price.
