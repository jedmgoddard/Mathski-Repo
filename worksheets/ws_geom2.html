Question 3
A geometric series has first term 12 and sum to infinity 20. Find the common ratio.
Formula
\(\dfrac{12}{1-r}=20\Rightarrow 12=20-20r\Rightarrow r=\dfrac{2}{5}\)
Answer
\(r=\dfrac{2}{5}\)
