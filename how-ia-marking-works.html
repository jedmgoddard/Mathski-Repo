How IA marking works

Last updated 22 October 2026

Mathski marks your IB Maths exploration against the five IB assessment criteria and tells you how secure each mark is.

What it marks

CriterionWhat it looks atMarks
APresentation0–4
BMathematical communication0–4
CPersonal engagement0–3
DReflection0–3
EUse of mathematics0–6

That gives a total out of 20. It works for all four courses: AA SL, AA HL, AI SL and AI HL.

How it works

  1. Upload your IA as a PDF and choose your course.
  2. See your scores straight away, free. You get a mark for each criterion with a verdict: secure, borderline or vulnerable. That tells you how likely the mark is to hold up if it were checked.
  3. Get the full report by email. It explains the reasoning for each criterion and what would move you up a band. Your first report is free; after that a report costs £3.49.

Teachers on the £49.99 plan get unlimited marking for their classes, plus coversheet comments they can edit.

How it is calibrated

The marking is tuned against a set of real IAs marked by experienced IB teachers, including moderated samples, across all four courses. It is deliberately strict: it rewards what is correct and present in the work, not ambition or the choice of topic. The aim is a mark that would hold up at moderation, not a flattering one.

What it can't do

  • It is guidance, not an official mark. Your teacher awards your IA mark, and the IB moderates it.
  • It can make mistakes, especially with handwriting, images of working, or unusual layouts. Use it as a second opinion.
  • It works best with a text-based PDF under 4.5 MB. Compress large files before uploading.

Academic honesty

Mathski gives feedback on work you have written. It does not write any part of your IA for you. Follow your school's rules on using feedback tools, and declare any use your school or the IB requires.

Your work

Your IA is sent to our AI provider to be marked and is not kept by Mathski afterwards. See our privacy policy for details.
